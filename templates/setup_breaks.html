Break Scheduling

Configure the planned start time, length, and description of playoff breaks. Saving a timing change shifts the scheduled times of subsequent, unplayed playoff matches and breaks to match the new end time.

The on-field break countdown starts after the preceding match is completed and committed, not automatically when the scheduled start time arrives.
